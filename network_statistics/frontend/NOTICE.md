# Map data

The bundled `world-map.svg` is an equirectangular conversion of Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, in the public domain.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Downloaded GeoJSON SHA-256: `9e0729ee253ca7d7a5c4ae9395fb1902264c5377c52e224d13dd85010e2835d9`. Longitude/latitude are projected to a 960 × 480 viewBox, rounded to one decimal place. No geolocation data is bundled in this land-outline asset.

# Optional IP location data

The opt-in updater downloads DB-IP City Lite separately from the repository.
The dataset is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Pages displaying its locations include the required linked attribution:
[IP Geolocation by DB-IP](https://db-ip.com).

Source and coverage: https://db-ip.com/db/download/ip-to-city-lite
No real IP geolocation records are bundled with the application or its preview.
